The name

Wisdom comes through many counselors

Counsel, not council. The name is about advice and guidance, not a committee. It comes from a thread that runs through Proverbs: plans and lives hold up better when they are shaped by many wise voices.

“Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.”

Proverbs 11:14 · KJV

“Without counsel purposes are disappointed: but in the multitude of counsellors they are established.”

Proverbs 15:22 · KJV

“For by wise counsel thou shalt make thy war: and in multitude of counsellors there is safety.”

Proverbs 24:6 · KJV

Process and purpose

Two names, one vision

The show began as Well Done. That name hasn't gone anywhere. It is now the reason behind every conversation.

The process

Counsel of Many

Seek wisdom, guidance and perspective through honest conversation with many people: different ages, callings, stories and convictions.

The purpose

Well Done

Live a life of faithfulness to Jesus Christ, and aim for the only review that finally matters.

“Well done, good and faithful servant.” Matthew 25:21

Seek counsel from many, discern what is true, and live for the ultimate “Well done.”

The posture

A learner at the table

I don't host this show as someone with all the answers. I'm here to ask good questions and listen closely to what people have learned through their lives. Many of them are people you might never get the chance to meet or question yourself.

The show stands on a Christian, biblical worldview. Guests don't have to share it. Not every view is equally true, but almost everyone has something worth learning.

  • CuriosityAsk what people have actually lived, not only what they think.
  • HumilityCome to learn, not to win the conversation.
  • DiscernmentWeigh every word against Scripture. Keep what is true.
