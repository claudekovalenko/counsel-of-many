Why the table is round

Most shows have a host and a guest. This one has a table. Every episode starts with a single question and brings in people who see it differently: friends, experts, strangers, sometimes the listeners themselves.

Nobody wins. The aim is to leave with a better question than the one you came in with.

The show started life on Spotify as Well Done. Same hosts, same spirit, and now a name that says what it actually is.
